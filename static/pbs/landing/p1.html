About the Conference

Python by the Sea brings together Python developers, data scientists, web developers, and enthusiasts for three days of learning, networking, and inspiration.

Set in the stunning De La Warr Pavilion overlooking the English Channel, this conference offers a unique blend of technical excellence and seaside charm. Whether you're a seasoned Python veteran or just starting your journey, you'll find talks, workshops, and connections that advance your skills and career.

Join us for keynotes from Python luminaries, deep-dive technical sessions, hands-on workshops, and plenty of opportunities to connect with fellow developers while enjoying the beautiful coastal setting of Bexhill-on-Sea.
