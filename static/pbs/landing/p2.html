The De La Warr Pavilion

Our venue is the stunning De La Warr Pavilion, a Grade I listed Modernist building right on Bexhill's seafront. Built in 1935, this architectural masterpiece offers inspiring spaces for learning and networking, with panoramic views of the English Channel.

Between sessions, step outside onto the terrace or take a walk along the beach. The pavilion's café and restaurant provide great spots for informal discussions and networking.
